## Must-fix

**Right-aligned buttons sit on the light end of the gradient, and "See fees" fails contrast**
- **Evidence:** `hero.css:4` right-aligns the buttons (`justify-content: flex-end`), which puts them over the lightest color, `#A78BFA`. "See fees" is the rightmost one, and `hero.css:7` gives it white text on a 25% white fill.
  - White on `#A78BFA` alone is about 2.7:1.
  - White on the 25% white fill over `#A78BFA` is about 2.1:1.
  - Body-size text needs 4.5:1, so this fails. Brand guideline line 6 requires AA on the gradient, so the brand rules call for this fix too.
- **Impact:** People with low vision may not be able to read one of the two actions. The layout is also split: the heading and description are on the left and the buttons are pushed to the far right, away from where people are reading.
- **Fix:**
  - Left-align the buttons (`justify-content: flex-start`) so they sit over the darker end, next to the text.
  - Give the secondary button a darker fill, such as `rgba(46,16,101,.45)` or solid `#5B21B6` with a 1px white border, instead of the 25% white fill.
- **Verify:** Measure each button's actual background color at desktop and 375px width. The text should reach at least 4.5:1.

For reference, the other combinations: white on `#5B21B6` is about 9.0:1, white on `#7C3AED` is about 5.7:1, and the primary button (`#5B21B6` on white) is about 9.0:1. All of those pass.

## Should-fix

**The description text may fail contrast on phones (likely, not rendered).** On narrow screens the gradient squeezes into the same width as the text. The right end of each line of `.sub` then lands on lighter purple, around 3:1. Check this at 375px. If it fails, you have two options:
- Make only the lighter end of the gradient's range narrower on small screens, for example by setting `background-size` so `#A78BFA` falls past the text. This is a judgment call against the brand rule.
- Add a subtle darkening layer behind the text.

**There is no layout for small screens (likely, not rendered).** There are no media queries.
- The heading stays at 56px and the sides keep 48px of padding, so a 375px screen has about 279px for text.
- The heading wraps to roughly 6–7 lines, and the button row doesn't wrap (`flex-wrap` isn't set).
- The two buttons need about 366px side by side, so they will likely squash, wrap their labels inside the pills, or overflow.
- Fix: use `font-size: clamp(2.25rem, 6vw, 3.5rem)`, set `flex-wrap: wrap` on `.ctas`, and make the padding smaller on small screens.

**The buttons have no hover or keyboard-focus styles** (`hero.css:5-7`). The browser's default focus ring on the purple varies by browser and can be hard to see near the light end.
- Add a `:focus-visible` style with enough contrast on every part of the gradient, such as a white outline plus a dark outer ring (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 4px #2E1065`).
- Add a hover state that doesn't depend on color alone.
