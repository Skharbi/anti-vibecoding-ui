## Must fix before launch

**1. The newsletter signup is fake, so you lose every lead**
- **Where:** `index.html:21-23`
- **What happens:** `subscribe()` shows `alert('Subscribed!')` and sends nothing. The input has no `name`, so pressing Enter just reloads the page. The Subscribe button is a `<div>` with `onclick`, so a keyboard user can't reach it and screen readers don't announce it as a button. The input only has a placeholder, not a label. Nothing checks the email format or reports an error.
- **Fix:** Use a real `<form action=… method="post">` with a `<label>`, `name="email"` and `required`, and a `<button type="submit">`. Show success or failure based on the actual server response, in a message screen readers will announce. Add a link to your privacy policy, since you're collecting email addresses.

**2. The customer numbers and quote look made up**
- **Where:** `index.html:18`, "Trusted by 10,000+ teams worldwide" and "Samplyr changed everything." — Happy Customer
- **Why it matters:** For a product that hasn't launched, this is probably untrue. An anonymous quote with no attribution is an obvious warning sign to scientists, and invented testimonials can also get you in trouble with regulators such as the FTC.
- **Fix:** Remove the section. If you have real pilot labs, name them with their permission. Otherwise, use something honest like "Now onboarding early-access labs."

**3. Nothing on the page says what Samplyr does**
- **Where:** `index.html:7-8, 13-15`, e.g. "Transform Your Workflow with AI-Powered Innovation", "Lightning Fast", "Smart insights that drive results"
- **Why it matters:** A lab manager can't tell this is for samples, freezers or labs. The page never mentions any of them. If you swapped in another product's name, the page would work just as well for it.
- **Fix:** Build the page around what the product actually does. For example: where each sample is (freezer, rack, box, position), who handled it and when, and barcode printing and scanning. Only list features that really exist.

**4. The security and "AI" claims can't be backed up**
- **Where:** `index.html:14-15`, "Enterprise-grade security you can trust" and "AI-Powered"
- **Why it matters:** Biotech buyers will ask about audit trails, data ownership, where data is hosted, and possibly 21 CFR Part 11 or GLP. A generic security claim makes them more suspicious, not less. If the product doesn't use AI, drop "AI-Powered". If it does, say exactly what the AI does.
- **Fix:** Say specific, verifiable things, or leave it out until you can.

**5. The layout breaks on phones**
- **Where:** `index.html:3` has no `<meta name="viewport">`, and `style.css` has no media queries.
- **What happens:** Phones render the page at desktop width and shrink it down. Even with a viewport tag, the 72px heading (`style.css:5`), the fixed three-column grid (`:8`) and the 64px side margins (`:2, 4, 6`) would overflow a 375px screen.
- **Fix:** Add the viewport tag and use `clamp()` for the heading size. Stack the feature grid into one column below about 720px. Cut the side margins to about 16px on phones.

**6. Most text is too faint to read comfortably**
- **Where:** `style.css:12` makes body text 60% white. On the blue end of the background with the glass overlay, that's about 2.5:1 contrast. WCAG AA requires 4.5:1.
- **Also:** Nav links (`:3`) are 70% white. White text on the `#3b82f6` end of the button gradient is about 3.7:1, which fails at 16px.
- **Fix:** Use solid, high-contrast text colors.

**7. The "Pricing" link goes nowhere**
- **Where:** `index.html:5` links to `#pricing`, but the page has no element with that id.
- **Related:** "Contact" leads to a newsletter form, and there's no actual way to contact you.
- **Fix:** Add a pricing section or remove the link. Give people a real contact route (email address or form), because small labs usually want to talk to someone before they buy.

## Should fix

  - There's no `<main>` or `<footer>`, and no footer links to privacy, terms, or company or contact details.
  - The features section has `<h3>` headings with no `<h2>` above them, which breaks the heading order.
  - The logo isn't a link back to the top of the page.
  - Screen readers will read the emoji icons aloud as "high voltage" and "sparkles". Hide them from screen readers or replace them.
- **Two different calls to action go to the same place:** "Get started →" and "Start your journey →" both link to `/signup`. Use one clear label that says what happens next, e.g. "Start free trial" or "Book a demo". Also confirm `/signup` actually works before launch; I couldn't check it from here.

- **Missing page metadata:** There's no meta description, no social preview tags (Open Graph), no favicon, and the `<title>` is just "Samplyr". A better title would be something like "Samplyr: Sample tracking for small biotech labs".
