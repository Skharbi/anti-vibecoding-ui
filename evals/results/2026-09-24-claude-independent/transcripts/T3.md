## final
I rewrote the modal so keyboard users can use it. I haven't run it: the repo has no `package.json`, build or tests, so nothing was type-checked or tried in a browser.

**Why keyboard users got stuck** (`src/ConfirmModal.tsx`, `src/modal.css`):
1. **Cancel, Archive and ✕ were `<div>`s and a `<span>`.** You can't Tab to them, and Enter/Space do nothing, so there was no way to close the modal from the keyboard.
2. **Focus stayed behind the modal.** Opening it didn't move focus into it, and Tab kept moving through the page underneath.
3. **Escape didn't close it.**
4. **When it closed, focus wasn't sent back** to the "Archive project" button.
5. **`.btn:focus, .close:focus { outline: none }`** hid the focus ring, so even a focused control would have been invisible.
6. Screen readers weren't told it's a dialog, the title wasn't a heading, and ✕ had no label.

**What I changed:**
- **Switched to the browser's built-in `<dialog>` opened with `showModal()`.** This gives you three things for free: the rest of the page can't be clicked or tabbed to while it's open, Escape closes it, and it's announced to screen readers as a dialog. It's labelled by its heading and described by the paragraph.
- **All three controls are real `<button type="button">`s.** ✕ is labelled "Close", with the symbol itself hidden from screen readers.
- **Focus goes to Cancel when the modal opens**, so an accidental Enter doesn't archive the project. It returns to the "Archive project" button when the modal closes, however it was closed.
- **Clicking outside still closes it.** The content now sits in a `.modal-body` wrapper so a click on the dark area can be told apart from a click inside the box.
- **CSS:** there's now a visible `:focus-visible` outline (it only shows for keyboard focus, not mouse clicks). I styled the dark background through `::backdrop` and removed `.overlay`. The width can't go past the screen edge on phones (`min(420px, 100vw - 32px)`), and the ✕ button is at least 24×24px.

What happens on Archive is unchanged: it still calls `onArchive()` and closes.

**To check by hand:** Tab to "Archive project" and press Enter, and focus should land on Cancel. Tab should cycle only through Cancel, Archive and ✕. Escape should close the modal and put focus back on the trigger. Also check it on a narrow screen.

**Not changed:** if `onArchive` is async, the modal closes right away without waiting for it, so failures and loading aren't handled. If it can fail, the modal should show a pending state and an error message.
