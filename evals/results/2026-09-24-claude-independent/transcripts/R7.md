# Hero review: `src/Hero.tsx` and `src/hero.css`

**Result: Fail.** Two things block the release: some text is too hard to read, and the page probably breaks on phones. Both can be fixed without breaking the brand rules.

## I did not flag the gradient or the pill buttons

A purple gradient with rounded pill buttons usually looks generic. Here, `docs/brand-guidelines.md` says the "Lumina sweep" gradient must be on the homepage hero (line 3), and says the pills are a trademarked part of the brand (line 4). Both follow the guidelines exactly, so I'm not asking you to change them. The same guidelines require text on the gradient to meet WCAG 2.2 AA contrast (line 6), and parts of this hero fail that.

## Must-fix

**1. The buttons sit on the lightest part of the gradient, and the "See fees" button can't be read**
- **Where:** `hero.css:4` pushes the buttons to the right edge (`justify-content: flex-end`). The gradient runs left to right and ends on its lightest color, `#A78BFA`.
- **Numbers:**
  - White text on `#A78BFA`: about 2.7:1.
  - "See fees" puts white text on a 25% white fill over that color (`hero.css:7`), which comes out around `#BDA8FB`: about 2.1:1.
  - Normal-size text needs 4.5:1, and even large text needs 3:1.
- **Impact:** the button for your second key action ("See fees") is barely legible, and this breaks your own brand rule on contrast.
- **Fix:**
  - Line the buttons up on the left under the subheading, so the text and buttons read as one column over the darker part of the gradient.
  - Give the secondary button a solid `#5B21B6` fill with white text (about 9:1). It stays a pill and uses a brand color.
- **Verify:** check contrast on the rendered button at 360, 768 and 1440px wide.

**2. On small screens the gradient squeezes under the text, and the headline likely overflows**
- **Where:** `hero.css:1–3`. The side padding is a fixed 48px and the headline is a fixed 56px. On a 360px phone that leaves about 264px for text.
- **Impact, part 1:** at that width the gradient is squeezed under the text. The right-hand part of the subheading lands on lighter purple, where white text falls below 4.5:1. The midpoint `#7C3AED` passes at about 5.7:1, but the color is below 4.5:1 from somewhere past the midpoint to the edge.
- **Impact, part 2:** "countries," at 56px is probably wider than 264px, so the headline likely spills out sideways.
- **Fix:**
  - `h1 { font-size: clamp(2.25rem, 6vw + 1rem, 3.5rem); overflow-wrap: break-word; }`
  - `padding: clamp(56px, 10vw, 96px) clamp(16px, 5vw, 48px);`
  - Use the extra space so all text stays roughly within the left 60% of the section.
- **Verify:** at 320 and 360px, the page should not scroll sideways and the rightmost characters should still pass contrast.

## Should-fix

**3. Buttons have no focus or hover styles** (`hero.css:5–7`)
- The browser's default focus ring may be hard to see on purple.
- **Fix:** `.pill:focus-visible { outline: 3px solid #fff; outline-offset: 3px; }`, plus hover and pressed states.

**4. On wide screens the buttons drift away from the text**
- Nothing caps the content width, so at 1920px the headline sits far left and the buttons far right. Fix #1 solves this. Also add a max-width container that matches the rest of the site.

**5. Class names are generic and apply site-wide** (`.sub`, `.pill`, `.primary`, `.secondary`)
- These will clash with styles elsewhere on the site.
- **Fix:** scope them, e.g. `.hero__cta--primary`, or use CSS modules.
- `color: #fff` on `.sub` (line 3) is also redundant, because it already inherits white.
