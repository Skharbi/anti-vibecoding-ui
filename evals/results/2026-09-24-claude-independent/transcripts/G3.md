## final
I built the insurance authorization form as `index.html`, `styles.css` and `app.js`, in plain HTML, CSS and JS as the README asks. It runs in demo mode for now: nothing is sent anywhere, and a yellow banner at the top of the page says so. I tested it in Chromium at 375px wide and at desktop width in dark mode. The whole flow works, and the only console error is a missing favicon. Nothing is committed.

**What I assumed:** "Insurance authorization" is the form a patient fills in on their phone at check-in so a clinic can bill their insurer. The clinic, Alder Creek Family Medicine, is made up. If you meant something else, such as a request for a treatment to be approved in advance, tell me and I'll change it.

**The flow:** four steps (Patient, Coverage, Consent, Review), then a confirmation screen.
- **Patient:** name, date of birth and mobile phone. The date of birth is three number boxes (month, day, year) rather than a date picker, because date pickers are slow for birth dates.
- **Coverage:** asks who the policyholder is. If it's not the patient, it also asks for that person's name and date of birth. Then the insurance company, member ID and an optional group number. A "Where do I find these?" section shows a drawing of an insurance card.
- **Consent:** three separate checkboxes: the insurer pays the clinic directly, the clinic can share claim information with the insurer, and the patient pays what insurance doesn't cover. It says up front that someone who won't agree can pay for the visit themselves. They sign by typing their name.
- **Review:** a summary with "Change" links. After editing a step, the button says "Save and return to review" and goes straight back.

**How it behaves on a phone:**
- The Back and Continue buttons stay pinned to the bottom of the screen.
- Text is large enough that iPhones don't zoom in when a field is tapped, and buttons and options are at least 44px tall.
- The phone's own back button moves back one step instead of leaving the form. Going forward again can't skip a step that isn't filled in correctly.
- Lighter blocks of text sit in paler grey, and dark mode is supported.

**Errors and checks:**
- Errors appear once someone leaves a field or presses Continue, not while they type. They show beside each field and in a list at the top of the step; tapping an error in that list jumps to the field.
- If the patient is under 18, "The patient" can't be chosen as the signer.
- Submission failures are handled: no connection, timeout, server error, expired link, and the server rejecting a specific answer. In that last case the form goes back to the right step with the error on that field.
- Retrying can't create a duplicate submission.

**Privacy:** nothing is saved in the browser. After submitting, the form is cleared and the confirmation screen says to hand a shared device back to the front desk. The page is also kept out of search results.

**Before going live:**
- Set `DEMO_MODE = false` in `app.js`. The form will then send its answers to `POST /api/insurance-authorizations`. That endpoint doesn't exist yet. It should return a `reference` when it succeeds, and list the problem fields when it rejects an answer.
- The checks in the browser are only there to help people fill the form in. The server must re-check everything, confirm the person is allowed to submit, and record the official signing time.
- A lawyer should approve the consent wording and the typed-name signature.
- To preview the failure screens, add `?simulate=network`, `timeout`, `server`, `session` or `invalid` to the address.
