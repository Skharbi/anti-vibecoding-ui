## Final output

I reworked the dashboard's layout and hierarchy in `src/Dashboard.tsx` and `src/dashboard.css`. I haven't rendered it or tested it at different screen widths, so it's unverified visually.

**What was wrong**
- Every text element was 15px/600, so nothing stood out. The KPI value was 16px, barely bigger than its label.
- Spacing values were arbitrary (13, 22, 7, 30, 27, 11, 41px) and ignored the existing `tokens.css`, which was never imported.
- The three buttons looked identical, so "New work order" didn't stand out as the main action.
- The "Recent activity" text was `#aaa` on white, which has low contrast.
- The layout was all `div`s, with no headings or landmarks.
- The fixed 4-column KPI grid would not reflow on narrow screens.

**What changed**
- **Hierarchy:**
  - The page title is 28px.
  - Section headings are 20px.
  - KPI values are 28px with tabular numerals.
  - KPI labels and deltas are 13px in muted grey.
- **Header:**
  - The title sits on the left and the buttons on the right.
  - "New work order" is the only filled button. Export and Settings are outlined.
  - Buttons are at least 40px tall, have hover states and a visible keyboard focus outline, and are `type="button"`.
- **Layout:**
  - The page is centred with a 1200px maximum width.
  - Spacing now uses the existing tokens, on a consistent 16/32px rhythm.
  - The KPI grid reflows by itself, with columns at least 200px wide.
- **Overdue inspections:** The list is now a bordered, divided card with a red left edge from `--color-danger`, so it reads as an alert rather than plain text.
- **Accessibility and semantics:**
  - Markup now uses `main`, `header`, `h1`, `h2` and `section` elements.
  - Muted text uses `--color-muted` (`#5f6368`), which has enough contrast.
  - The component's props and content are unchanged.
