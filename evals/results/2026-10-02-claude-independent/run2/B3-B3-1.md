**[Must-fix] Focus doesn't move into the dialog when it opens**
- Evidence: `ShareDialog.tsx:8-9`. There's no `autoFocus`, ref or effect.
- Impact: Focus stays on "Share report" behind the dialog. Keyboard and screen-reader users have to tab through the rest of the page to reach the dialog.
- Fix: Focus the email input or the dialog on open.

**[Must-fix] There's no focus containment and the background stays interactive**
- Evidence: `ShareDialog.tsx:9`. The `role="dialog"` has no `aria-modal`, no focus trap, no `inert` on the background and no backdrop. `dialog.css:1` is just a fixed box.
- Impact: Tab leaves the dialog into the page behind it, and the page can still be clicked.
- Fix: Use the native `<dialog>` with `showModal()`, which gives you the trap, inert background, Escape and a backdrop. Otherwise add `aria-modal` plus `inert` and a trap.

**[Must-fix] Escape doesn't close it, and focus isn't returned on close**
- Evidence: There's no key handler, and the trigger button has no ref to restore focus to (`ShareDialog.tsx:7,13-14`).
- Impact: Keyboard users lose their place when the dialog closes. Escape doing nothing is a standard dismissal gap.
- Fix: `showModal()` handles Escape. Also restore focus to the trigger on close.

**[Must-fix] "Send" doesn't send anything**
- Evidence: `ShareDialog.tsx:13`. `onClick` only calls `setOpen(false)`. There's no form, no submit handler and no use of the input value. The input is also uncontrolled and never read.
- Impact: Users are told nothing and get no confirmation or error, yet the dialog closes as if it worked. That's misleading feedback on the primary task.
- Fix: Wrap the fields in a `<form>` with a submit handler. Add pending, success and error states, and keep the value on error. Alternatively, if this is a stub, don't ship it as finished.

**[Should-fix] Narrow and short viewports**
- Evidence: `dialog.css:1`. There's no `max-width`, `max-height` or `overflow`. It's `position: fixed` with `top: 20%` and `translateX(-50%)`.
- Impact: It may clip or overflow at 320px wide or when the on-screen keyboard is open. I haven't rendered it, so this is **Likely**, not confirmed.
- Fix: Add `max-width: calc(100vw - 32px)`, `max-height` and `overflow: auto`, then check at 320px.

**[Should-fix] Form field details**
- Evidence: `ShareDialog.tsx:12`. The email input has no `required`, `autocomplete` or `name`, and nothing to show an error.
