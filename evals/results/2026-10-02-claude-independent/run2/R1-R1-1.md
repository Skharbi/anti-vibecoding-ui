# Samplyr landing page review

**Result: Fail.** There are confirmed must-fix defects in `index.html` and `style.css`. I read the code only and didn't render it in a browser, so contrast figures are estimates and nothing was tested on a device or with a keyboard.

## Must-fix

**1. The Subscribe button isn't a button, and it reports success without doing anything**
- Evidence: `index.html:21` has `<div class="pill" onclick="subscribe()">`. `index.html:23` defines `subscribe()` as `alert('Subscribed!')`.
- Impact:
  - Keyboard and screen-reader users can't reach or activate it, because a `div` isn't focusable and has no role.
  - Pressing Enter in the field does nothing, since the form has no submit control.
  - Nothing is sent anywhere. Every visitor is told they subscribed and no email is captured.
- Fix: use `<button type="submit">` and give the form a real `action` or fetch handler. Replace `alert` with inline status text, such as "Subscribed" or an error with a retry.
- Verify: Tab to the button, press Enter, and confirm the request reaches your backend. Then test the failure path.

**2. The email input has no label**
- Evidence: `index.html:21` has `<input type="email" placeholder="Your email">`. It has no `<label>`, `name`, `autocomplete="email"` or `required`.
- Impact: there's no persistent accessible name, and browsers can't autofill it reliably.
- Fix: add a visible label, `name="email"`, `autocomplete="email"` and `required`. Add a line saying what they're signing up for and how often you'll write.

**3. The page breaks on mobile**
- Evidence:
  - There's no `<meta name="viewport">` in `index.html:3`, so phones render at desktop width.
  - `style.css` has no media queries at all.
  - The h1 is fixed at 72px (`:5`).
  - The hero and nav use 64px side padding (`:2`, `:4`).
  - The features grid is a fixed 3 columns (`:8`).
  - The nav has four links plus a pill, with 32px gaps and no wrapping (`:2`).
- Impact: this will overflow horizontally at 390px and 320px, and the nav will be unusable.
- Fix:
  - Add the viewport meta tag.
  - Use `clamp()` for the headline size.
  - Use `repeat(auto-fit, minmax(240px, 1fr))` for the grid.
  - Reduce padding below about 640px.
  - Let the nav wrap or collapse.
- Verify: check at 320, 390 and 768px with no page-level horizontal scroll.

**4. Body text and buttons likely fail contrast**
- Evidence:
  - Paragraphs are `rgba(255,255,255,.6)` (`style.css:12`) and nav links are `.7` (`:3`), both on a purple-to-blue gradient.
  - My rough estimate for the paragraph text over the blue end is about 2.8:1, against the 4.5:1 target.
  - White text on the pill gradient (`#8b5cf6` to `#3b82f6`) is also likely under 4.5:1.
- Fix: use solid, opaque text colors on a flat background and measure them with a contrast tool. Please confirm the exact ratios in a browser.

**5. Dead and mislabeled navigation**
- Evidence:
  - `#pricing` (`index.html:5`) has no matching element, so the link does nothing.
  - "Contact" jumps to the newsletter form, not a contact method.
  - `/signup` is an absolute path. I can't see that route in this repo.
- Fix: add a pricing section or remove the link, and rename or replace "Contact". Confirm `/signup` resolves in production.

## Should-fix

- **The page never says what Samplyr does.** The words sample, lab, freezer, biotech, chain of custody and barcode don't appear anywhere. The headline and sub-headline (`:7-8`) would fit any software product. This is the most important copy fix, ahead of the styling. Lead with the real task, for example what you track, for whom, and what it replaces.
- **Generic AI-template styling.**
  - Purple-to-blue gradient background.
  - Gradient headline text.
  - Glass cards with glow, nested inside another glass card (`:11`).
  - A 3-card icon trio with emoji icons (`:13-15`).
  - Pill buttons with arrows.
  - Together they make the page look like thousands of other products. The bigger problem is that none of it shows the product.
  - Replace the trio with a real product screenshot or a sample-record view. Use a flat, readable palette.
- **The gradient text can disappear.**
  - `style.css:5` sets `color: transparent` with only the `-webkit-` prefixed `background-clip`.
  - If the clip isn't applied, or in forced-colors mode, the headline can vanish. This is a reason to drop the effect.
- **Emoji used as icons** (`:13-15`) aren't hidden from screen readers. Mark them `aria-hidden` or replace them.
- **Semantics and structure:**
  - There's no `<main>` landmark.
  - The nav isn't a list.
  - The pill-styled links need clear focus styles (check this in a browser).
  - The page has no footer, privacy link, or any real way to contact you.
- **Discoverability:**
  - `<title>` is just "Samplyr".
  - There's no meta description, favicon or social/share metadata.
  - Give the title a descriptive phrase, for example "Samplyr: sample tracking for small biotech labs".
- **Email collection has no notice.** There's no statement of purpose or privacy link. I'm not making a legal claim, but add a short notice and check what your jurisdiction needs.
- **Minor:**
  - `"Inter"` is named but never loaded, so users get the fallback sans-serif.
  - `backdrop-filter` on several large layers is a potential cost on low-end devices.
