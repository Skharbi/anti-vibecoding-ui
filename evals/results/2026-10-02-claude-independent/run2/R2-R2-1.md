## Must-fix

**1. The core controls can't be used with a keyboard or screen reader**
- Evidence: `src/Signup.tsx:14`, `:15` and `:21` use `<div onClick>` for "View terms", "Sign up" and "Close". `signup.css:4` also removes the focus outline (`outline: none`) on the input, `.cta` and `.link`.
- Impact: Tab skips all three controls, and a screen reader doesn't announce them as buttons. Nobody using a keyboard can sign up or open the terms. Even if they could focus the input, they couldn't see where focus is. Enter in the email field does nothing.
- Fix:
  - Wrap the fields in `<form onSubmit>`.
  - Make "Sign up" a `<button type="submit">`.
  - Make "View terms" and "Close" `<button type="button">`.
  - Delete the `outline: none` rule and add a visible `:focus-visible` style.
- Verify: Tab through the form, press Enter in the email field to submit, and check that every control shows a focus ring.

**2. The terms modal has no dialog behavior**
- Evidence: `Signup.tsx:16-23` is plain divs. There is no `role="dialog"` or `aria-modal`, no accessible name, no focus move on open or return on close, no focus trap, and no Escape handler.
- Impact: Focus stays behind the backdrop, so keyboard users can tab into the covered form. Screen readers don't announce a dialog. There's also no way to dismiss it with Escape.
- Fix: Use `<dialog>` with `showModal()` (this gives you the trap, Escape and focus return for free). Name it with `aria-labelledby` pointing at the heading. Add `max-height` and overflow handling so it fits small screens.
- Verify: Open the modal with the keyboard, confirm focus lands inside, Tab stays inside, Escape closes it, and focus returns to "View terms".

**3. The email field has no label or error association, and a valid email does nothing**
- Evidence:
  - `Signup.tsx:11` has a `<label>` with no `htmlFor`, and the input has no `id`, `type="email"` or `autocomplete`.
  - `:13` renders the error as a bare div, with no `aria-invalid`, `aria-describedby` or live region.
  - `:15` only sets or clears an error. There is no submit, pending or success handling, and no terms-acceptance step even though the terms are shown.
- Impact: Screen readers don't name the field and never announce the error. A valid email gives no feedback at all, so the user can't tell whether signup worked.
- Fix:
  - Add `htmlFor`/`id`, `type="email"` and `autoComplete="email"`.
  - Add `aria-invalid` and `aria-describedby` pointing at the error, and render the error with `role="alert"`.
  - Wire up a real submit with a pending state, a success state and a failure state.
- Verify: Submit an invalid email and a valid one, and check that a screen reader announces the label and each result.
