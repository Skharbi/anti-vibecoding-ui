## Findings

**[Should-fix] "Close work order" has no confirmation, pending state, or feedback, and its name is ambiguous**
- Evidence: `WorkOrders.tsx:27` calls `onClose(wo.id)` immediately. Every card's button has the same accessible name, "Close work order". The Assign button on line 26 includes the asset name.
- Impact:
  - One misclick closes an order, and the button is right next to Assign with the same styling.
  - Screen reader users hear a list of identical buttons.
  - Nothing prevents a double submit, and there's no sign that the action worked.
  - When the card disappears, focus is lost to `<body>` (likely, depends on the parent).
- Fix:
  - Name it `Close {wo.asset}` or add `aria-label`/visually hidden text.
  - Add a confirm step or an undo toast, unless closing is reversible.
  - Disable the button while the action is pending.
  - Move focus to the next card or the heading after removal.
- Verify: check reversibility of close with the backend owner. Then tab through with a screen reader and close the last item.

**[Should-fix] Loading, error, and empty states drop the `<main>` and `<h1>`**
- Evidence: the early returns on lines 8–10 skip the `<main>`/`<h1>` on line 12.
- Impact:
  - Those states have no landmark and no page heading.
  - A refetch (`loading=true`) replaces the whole list with a text line, which causes layout jump and loses scroll position.
  - `role="status"` on a freshly mounted `<p>` may not be announced.
- Fix: always render `<main>` and `<h1>`, and swap only the body content. Keep stale items visible during a refetch, and keep the live region mounted.
- Verify: toggle each state and check the landmarks and heading.

**[Should-fix] Priority is barely distinguished, and the list has no triage tools**
- Evidence: `workorders.css:8` styles only `.prio-P1`, with a `▲` added through `content`. `.prio-P2` and `.prio-P3` have no rules. There is no sort, no filter, and no overdue indicator.
- Impact:
  - P2 and P3 look identical, and P1 differs by one glyph.
  - Some screen readers read the glyph aloud as "black up-pointing triangle".
  - Technicians can't scan by urgency or due date.
- Fix:
  - Make priority a styled badge with text, and keep the text as the carrier.
  - Hide the glyph from assistive tech, or use `content: "▲" / ""`.
  - Sort by priority then due date, or add a sort control.
  - Flag overdue items in text.
- Judgment call: whether to add filters depends on how many orders are typical.

**[Should-fix] Due date formatting depends on the browser's time zone and locale**
- Evidence: `WorkOrders.tsx:23` uses `new Date(wo.due).toLocaleDateString()` with no options.
- Impact: a date-only string like `2026-10-03` parses as UTC and can show as the previous day in US time zones. A bad value renders "Invalid Date".
- Needs verification: the format of `due` (date-only or full timestamp).
- Fix: format with `Intl.DateTimeFormat` and an explicit `timeZone` (the site's, or `"UTC"` for date-only values). Add a fallback for invalid values.

**[Should-fix] Raw `error` string is shown to users**
- Evidence: `WorkOrders.tsx:9` renders `{error}`.
- Impact: this may expose technical or server text. The message isn't actionable beyond "try again".
- Fix: show a mapped, friendly message and log the detail.
