## Hero review: `src/Hero.tsx` and `src/hero.css`

I read the code and computed contrast ratios from the CSS values. I did not render the page, so everything below is code-based, not observed in a browser.

**Overall: Fail.** There is one confirmed must-fix, and rendered checks are still outstanding.

### Must-fix

**[Must-fix] Secondary CTA text fails contrast, and it sits on the lightest part of the gradient**
Evidence: `hero.css:4` right-aligns the buttons with `justify-content: flex-end`. `hero.css:7` gives `.secondary` white text on `rgba(255,255,255,.25)`. `hero.css:1` runs the gradient to `#A78BFA` at the right edge.
- Computed contrast is about 2.1:1 over `#A78BFA` at the right edge. Even over the mid-stop `#7C3AED` it is only about 3.6:1. The WCAG AA requirement for 16px text is 4.5:1.
- This assumes the section spans the viewport width, which is the default for a block `section`. Confirm that in the browser.

Impact: "See fees" is hard to read for low-vision users and in sunlight. It is also the path to pricing, which is the main objection a freelancer has before signing up.
Fix: make the secondary button an opaque, darker surface or an outline. For example, use a 2px white border on a transparent background over a darkened gradient. Alternatively, drop the gradient for a solid `#5B21B6`-range background so every position passes.
Verify: sample the rendered background behind each button and check that text contrast is at least 4.5:1.

### Should-fix

**[Should-fix] Paragraph contrast depends on viewport width**
Evidence: `hero.css:3` sets `.sub` to white, and `hero.css:1` runs the gradient across the full section width.
- White on `#A78BFA` is about 2.7:1. On narrow screens the 52ch paragraph spans most of the gradient, so its right-hand text will land on the low-contrast end.
- This is likely but not confirmed, because the result depends on where the text actually sits when rendered.

Fix: use a gradient that stays dark enough everywhere, ending around `#6D28D9`, or use a solid colour. Then re-check at 320px and 768px.

**[Should-fix] CTAs are detached from the content they belong to**
Evidence: `hero.css:4` right-aligns the buttons while the h1 and paragraph are left-aligned.
Impact: the eye reads headline, then paragraph, then has to jump across the full width to find the action. On wide screens the buttons end up far from the copy.
Fix: remove `justify-content: flex-end` so the buttons align left under the paragraph. If they should wrap on mobile, add `flex-wrap: wrap`.

**[Should-fix] Fixed 56px heading and 48px side padding on small screens**
Evidence: `hero.css:1-2`. There are no media queries and no `clamp()`.
- At 320px the content width is 224px. Long words like "currency" at 56px are near that width, so overflow or very ragged wrapping is likely. This is not yet confirmed.

Fix: `font-size: clamp(2rem, 6vw, 3.5rem)` and `padding: clamp(48px, 10vw, 96px) clamp(20px, 5vw, 48px)`.
Verify: check at 320, 768 and 1280px for no horizontal scroll.

**[Should-fix] No hover, focus or active styles on the CTAs**
Evidence: `hero.css:5-7` defines no `:hover`, `:focus-visible` or `:active` rules.
- The browser default focus ring may be hard to see against the purple background, especially on the white button. Check this by tabbing through the page.

Fix: add an explicit `:focus-visible` outline with at least 3:1 contrast against both the button and the gradient, for example a 3px white outline with an offset. Add hover and active states too.

**[Should-fix] The visual treatment is generic**
Evidence: `hero.css:1` uses a purple linear gradient with a white pill button, and the layout is a centred-feel text block with no product visual.
- The copy is specific, but the look could belong to many fintech products. Showing a real invoice, an FX rate or a payout would make it Lumina's own.

### Judgment calls

- Whether the hero needs a product visual, such as a sample invoice or payout card. That depends on brand and assets.
