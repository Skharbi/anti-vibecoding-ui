## Must-fix (confirmed in code)

1. **Analytics loads before any consent.** `ConsentBanner.tsx:6` calls `loadAnalytics()` in a mount effect with no check. Google Tag Manager loads for every visitor, including those who haven't answered and those who would refuse. Under ePrivacy/GDPR-style regimes, non-essential trackers generally need consent first, so this is the biggest risk.
2. **There is no way to refuse or manage choices.** The only button is "Accept all" (`:11`). "Manage preferences" (`:12`) is a plain link to `/privacy`, not a preference control. There is no "Reject all" and no per-category choice. Regulators such as the CNIL and EDPB expect refusing to be about as easy as accepting. I haven't checked whether `/privacy` has a working settings panel.
3. **Consent is implied, not given.** The copy says "By continuing to browse you agree" (`:10`). Continuing to browse isn't an affirmative action, and the banner doesn't name the purposes (analytics, advertising) or the third parties.
4. **Choices aren't stored accurately, and withdrawing isn't possible.**
   - `localStorage "consent"` only ever holds `"all"`.
   - `loadAds()` only runs on the click. On a returning visit with `consent=all`, ads never load, so the stored state doesn't drive behaviour.
   - Once the banner is dismissed, nothing lets the user change their mind.
   - The consent record has no timestamp, policy version or expiry.
5. **The banner can't be used with a keyboard or screen reader reliably.** It is a `role="region"` with no focus handling, and it is `position: fixed` with no reserved space, so it can cover page content and focus targets. A fixed banner on a small screen with no `flex-wrap` can also crowd the content.
6. **Contrast failures** (my own calculation, so confirm with a tool):
   - "Manage preferences" is `#666` on `#111` at 11px. That is about 3.3:1 and needs 4.5:1. It is also tiny and visually de-emphasised, which works against the user's choice.
   - White on `#22c55e` for the Accept button is about 2.3:1.
7. **Smaller issues.**
   - `localStorage` is read directly in `useState` at `:5`. That throws if storage is blocked and breaks under SSR.
   - `.accept` and `.tiny` have no `:focus-visible` styles.
   - The link's touch target is well under 24px.

- Load nothing non-essential until consent is recorded.
- Offer equally prominent **Accept all** and **Reject all** buttons, plus a **Customize** option with per-category toggles that default to off.
- Store `{choices, timestamp, policyVersion}` in a try/catch wrapper.
- Load only the categories the user allowed, on mount and on change.
- Add a persistent "Cookie settings" link in the footer so users can withdraw consent.
- Reword the copy to name the purposes and drop the "by continuing" language.
- Fix contrast and focus styles, raise the link text to at least 14px, and test at 320px width.
