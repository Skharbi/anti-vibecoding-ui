## Must-fix

**[Must-fix] The full intake form, including health and ID data, is sent to a third-party analytics endpoint**
Evidence: `src/IntakeForm.tsx:12` calls `analytics.track("intake_submitted", { ...v, ts: Date.now() })`. `v` holds all eight fields: name, date of birth, phone, national ID, insurance number, symptoms, medications and pregnancy status. `src/analytics.ts:3` sends that object with `navigator.sendBeacon` to `https://collect.example-analytics.io/v1/e`, along with `location.href`.
Impact:
- On every submit, identity and health data leaves the app's trust boundary for a third party. The user is never told.
- The beacon is sent before `onSubmit` runs, so it goes out even if the real submission fails.
- `location.href` can also leak query-string or route data.
- Whether this breaches a specific regulation (HIPAA, GDPR, or local law) depends on jurisdiction and your agreements with the vendor. I'm not asserting that. It is a high privacy risk that your compliance owner should review.

Fix:
- Remove `...v` from the event. Send only non-identifying metadata, such as `{ ts }` or a form version.
- Make `track` allowlist its property keys, so a future caller can't pass form values by accident.
- Strip the query string and hash from `url`.
- Don't track on the intake route at all unless there is a documented need and a data-processing agreement.

Verify: submit the form with DevTools Network open and confirm the beacon payload has no field values. Add a unit test asserting that `track` is never called with form values.

## Should-fix

**[Should-fix] There is no error handling, pending state or success feedback on submit**
Evidence: `IntakeForm.tsx:10-14` runs `await onSubmit(v)` with no try/catch, no loading flag and no confirmation.
Impact:
- If `onSubmit` rejects, the promise goes unhandled and the user sees nothing, so they may believe a medical intake went through.
- The button stays active during the request, so a double-click can submit twice.
- `v` is never reset.

Fix:
- Add `status` state (`idle`, `submitting`, `error`, `success`).
- Disable the button while submitting.
- Show an error message in a container with `role="alert"` and keep the entered data on failure.
- Show a clear success message.

**[Should-fix] The form has no validation and no required or optional indication**
Evidence: none of the inputs has `required`, `aria-required`, a pattern, or error messaging. An empty form can be submitted.
Impact: incomplete intakes are possible. Server-side validation still has to be confirmed separately, because client checks aren't a security boundary.
Fix: mark the truly required fields and say which are optional. Add inline errors linked with `aria-describedby`, and move focus to the first invalid field or an error summary.

**[Should-fix] Pregnancy status is a free-text input**
Evidence: `IntakeForm.tsx:25` is a plain `<input>` bound to `pregnant`.
Impact: answers will be inconsistent ("yes", "Y", "12 wks", "n/a"). The field is also shown to every patient, with no explanation of why it is asked or how it is used.
Fix: use a radio group in a `fieldset` and `legend`, with options such as Yes, No, Not applicable and Prefer not to say. Whether to show it conditionally is a product and clinical decision.

**[Should-fix] Sensitive fields have no browser or input hints, and some autofill hints are missing**
Evidence:
- National ID and insurance number (`IntakeForm.tsx:21-22`) have no `autoComplete`, `inputMode` or `spellCheck={false}`.
- The symptoms and medications textareas (lines 23-24) also have no `autoComplete="off"`.

Impact: sensitive values can end up in browser autofill and spellcheck services, and mobile users get the wrong keyboard.
Fix: set `autoComplete="off"` on those fields (and on the pregnancy field when it is a radio), and `spellCheck={false}` on the ID fields. Add `inputMode` where the format is known. Consider `autoComplete="tel"` plus a format hint on the phone field.

**[Should-fix] There is no field grouping, help text, or purpose or privacy statement**
Evidence: a flat list of `label` and `input` pairs with no `fieldset`, headings or helper text.
Impact:
- Patients aren't told why a national ID is needed, who sees the data, or what happens next.
- Without grouping, the form is harder to scan and to navigate with a screen reader.

Fix:
- Group the fields under "Personal details", "Insurance and ID" and "Health information".
- Add a short note on why the data is collected and who can see it.
- Link to your privacy notice if you have one.

**[Should-fix] State updates use a stale closure**
Evidence: `IntakeForm.tsx:8` uses `setV({ ...v, [k]: ... })` rather than the functional form.
Impact: fast successive updates, such as autofill filling several fields at once, can overwrite each other. This is likely rather than confirmed.
Fix: use `setV(prev => ({ ...prev, [k]: e.target.value }))`.

## Judgment calls

- **Terse IDs:** `id="n"`, `"d"`, `"p"` and so on work but are hard to maintain. Use descriptive IDs, or `useId`.
- **Styling:** the form has no layout or styles, and no CSS was provided to review. Focus visibility, touch target size, contrast and the 320px reflow can't be assessed.
- **Date of birth:** `type="date"` support and formatting vary by browser. Consider whether three separate fields suit your audience better.
