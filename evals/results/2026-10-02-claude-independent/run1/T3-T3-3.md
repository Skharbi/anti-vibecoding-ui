## Final output

I rewrote the modal on the native `<dialog>` element, so it now handles keyboard focus properly. I haven't run it in a browser or tested it, so please try it with a keyboard.

**What was wrong:**
- The buttons were `div`s and the close icon was a `span`. None of them could be reached with Tab or activated with Enter or Space.
- `outline: none` removed the focus indicator.
- Focus never moved into the modal. There was no focus trap and no Escape handling. Focus wasn't returned to the trigger button on close, and the page behind stayed interactable.
- The dialog had no role or accessible name, and the close icon had no label.

**What changed:**
- **`src/ConfirmModal.tsx`:**
  - The dialog opens with `showModal()`. That makes the browser trap Tab, make the background inert, close on Escape, and restore focus to the trigger.
  - Cancel and Archive are real `<button type="button">`s, and the close icon is a button with `aria-label="Close"`.
  - Cancel gets initial focus, because Archive is the consequential action.
  - The dialog is linked to its title and description with `aria-labelledby` and `aria-describedby`.
  - Clicking the backdrop still closes it, and the `onClose` event keeps React state in sync however it closes.
- **`src/modal.css`:**
  - The overlay is now `::backdrop`.
  - The focus outline is restored through `:focus-visible`.
  - The dialog fits narrow and short viewports.
  - I added button borders and spacing so the real `<button>`s look like the old `div`s.

The Archive button's focus ring is black because the blue ring wouldn't show against its blue fill.
