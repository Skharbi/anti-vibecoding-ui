## Must-fix

**[Must-fix] The full intake payload, including health and identity data, is sent to a third-party analytics endpoint**
Evidence: `src/IntakeForm.tsx:12` calls `analytics.track("intake_submitted", { ...v, ts })`. `v` holds every field: name, DOB, phone, national ID, insurance number, symptoms, medications and pregnancy status. `src/analytics.ts:3` sends that via `sendBeacon` to `https://collect.example-analytics.io/v1/e`, along with `location.href`. (Confirmed)
Impact: Health and identity data leaves the app for a third party, regardless of what the backend does. The URL may carry identifiers too. The call also runs before `onSubmit`, so the data goes out even if the real submission fails. I can't tell from the code whether the endpoint is a processor you have an agreement with, or what retention it has. That is for you to verify, and I'm not claiming a legal breach.
Fix: Remove the field spread. Track only a content-free event such as `intake_submitted` with no form values. Strip the query string and hash from `url`. Move the call to after `onSubmit` succeeds.
Verify: In the network tab, submit with test data and confirm no field values or identifiers reach the analytics host.

**[Must-fix] A failed or slow submission gives the user no feedback and no protection against double submits**
Evidence: `src/IntakeForm.tsx:10-14` awaits `onSubmit(v)` with no try/catch, no pending state, and no success state. The button at line 26 is never disabled. (Confirmed)
Impact: If `onSubmit` rejects, the user sees nothing and the error goes unhandled. On a slow connection they will click again and may send duplicate intakes. After a success the form also stays filled with the patient's data and there is no confirmation. For a medical intake, silent failure is a data-loss and safety risk.
Fix: Add `status` state (idle, submitting, error, success). Disable the button while submitting. Show an error in an `role="alert"` region that keeps the entered values and offers a retry. On success, clear the form and show a confirmation.
Verify: Make `onSubmit` reject, then delay it. Confirm the error appears, values are preserved, and a second submit can't be fired.

## Should-fix

- **No required fields, validation or inline errors** (`IntakeForm.tsx:18-26`). Nothing has `required`, `aria-required` or format checks, so a blank form submits. Add native `required` for name, DOB and phone, and error text linked with `aria-describedby`. Server-side validation still has to be confirmed separately.
- **Pregnancy status is free text** (`:25`). Free text invites inconsistent answers like "yes", "maybe" or "N/A", which is poor for a clinical field. Use radios (Yes / No / Not applicable / Prefer not to say) in a `fieldset` with a `legend`. Whether the field should exist at all for every patient is a product call.
- **Sensitive fields lack input hints** (`:21-22`, `:25`). National ID and insurance number have no `autoComplete="off"` (or an appropriate token), no `inputMode`, and no format hint. Text fields have no `maxLength`. They are also not marked as sensitive, so browsers may save and autofill them.
- **Cryptic IDs and no spacing or layout.** Ids like `n`, `d` and `p` work but are hard to maintain. Labels and inputs are siblings with no wrapper or grouping, so layout and spacing are unconstrained. Related fields (identity, insurance, clinical) aren't grouped in `fieldset`s, and nothing explains why each sensitive item is being collected. I couldn't check rendering, so treat the layout part as Needs verification.
- **No privacy notice or purpose statement.** The form collects national ID and health data with no visible explanation of use or consent. This is a verification item for your compliance owner, not a finding of non-compliance.
- **`set` closes over a stale `v`** (`:8`). `setV({ ...v, ... })` can drop edits when updates batch. Use the functional form `setV(p => ({ ...p, [k]: value }))`.
- **Textareas have no size or limit guidance** (`:23-24`), and the symptoms and medications fields have no help text. Add short hints and `maxLength`.
