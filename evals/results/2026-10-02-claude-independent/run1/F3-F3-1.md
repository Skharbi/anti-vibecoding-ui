**Fixed**

**Judgment calls, left unchanged**

- **Error announcement:** Errors use the polite `status` role. `role="alert"` would interrupt the user, which seems unnecessary for a retryable failure.
- **Fieldset border:** `#c4c7cc` is low contrast, but it is decoration. The legend is what identifies the group, so it isn't a WCAG failure.
